      <div class="reading-body"><section class="reading-section" id="rp-book-1-1-summary" aria-labelledby="rp-book-1-1-summary-title">
    <div class="section-title"><span>01</span><h2 id="rp-book-1-1-summary-title">What I read</h2></div>
    <p class="summary-text">Neilson reports on Molly Easterlin’s study of team sports and childhood adversity. Researchers followed adolescents into adulthood and found an association between team participation and fewer later symptoms or diagnoses of depression and anxiety among those who had experienced trauma. The article does not establish that sports alone caused this difference: family support and a sense of safety may also matter. Its central tension is access. Participation fees increasingly exclude lower-income children, who may have more adverse childhood experiences. Neilson uses the research to support a wider discussion about making team sports available to the young people who could benefit.</p>
      <aside class="reading-focus"><span class="eyebrow">READING FOCUS</span><p>Track barriers to participation. Pay-to-play fees and household income limit access; the article also connects lower educational attainment and single-parent households with financial disadvantage.</p></aside>
  </section><section class="reading-section" id="rp-book-1-1-evidence" aria-labelledby="rp-book-1-1-evidence-title">
    <div class="section-title"><span>02</span><h2 id="rp-book-1-1-evidence-title">Evidence &amp; analysis</h2></div>
    <article class="evidence-item">
    <span class="label-word">CLAIM</span><h3>Access is unequal.</h3>
    <p><span class="label-word">EVIDENCE · PARAPHRASED</span>Near the end, Neilson reports that lower-income children participate at half the rate of wealthier peers.</p>
    <p class="explain"><span class="label-word">WHY IT MATTERS</span>The contrast shifts attention from individual motivation to the cost of joining a team.</p>
  </article><article class="evidence-item">
    <span class="label-word">CLAIM</span><h3>Association needs careful interpretation.</h3>
    <p><span class="label-word">EVIDENCE · PARAPHRASED</span>In the discussion of the study’s limits, Christina Bethell points to family connections and safety.</p>
    <p class="explain"><span class="label-word">WHY IT MATTERS</span>These factors may support both wellbeing and sports participation. The study cannot isolate sports as the sole cause.</p>
  </article><article class="evidence-item">
    <span class="label-word">CLAIM</span><h3>The structure moves from a person to a public issue.</h3>
    <p><span class="label-word">EVIDENCE · PARAPHRASED</span>Easterlin’s childhood memories lead into longitudinal research and then policy proposals.</p>
    <p class="explain"><span class="label-word">WHY IT MATTERS</span>The personal opening makes the question approachable; the research and access gap give it wider significance.</p>
  </article>

    <span class="eyebrow">Perspective 01</span><h3>1994–95 · Adolescence</h3><p>Participants in grades 7–12 reported sports participation or plans to participate. The wider data set included 9,668 teens; the analysis focused on about 5,000 with at least one adverse childhood experience.</p>
  </article><article class="lab-panel" data-panel="1">
    <span class="eyebrow">Perspective 02</span><h3>2008 · Adulthood</h3><p>The same people were asked about diagnoses of depression or anxiety and assessed for depressive symptoms. Following people over time establishes a sequence, but is not the same as randomly assigning a treatment.</p>

    <span class="eyebrow">Perspective 03</span><h3>2019 · Reporting</h3><p>Neilson connects the findings to an access problem: children who might benefit can be excluded by cost. The article reports an association and calls for broader participation.</p>

    <div class="section-title"><span>05</span><h2 id="rp-book-1-1-reflection-title">My response</h2></div>
    <div class="written-response"><p>I would spend the first round of funding on waiving team fees and lending equipment. Neilson’s comparison of participation rates suggests that the cost of joining can decide who gets the chance to play. I would track whether more students join and stay on teams after the change, then ask which barriers remain. Higher participation would tell us whether access improved. The study’s association with adult mental health raises a separate question that a school’s enrollment figures cannot answer.</p></div>

<script>
(() => {
  'use strict';
  const root = document.getElementById('rp-book-1-1');
  if (!root || root.dataset.initialized === 'true') return;
  root.dataset.initialized = 'true';
  root.dataset.enhanced = 'true';
  const $ = selector => root.querySelector(selector);
  const $$ = selector => [...root.querySelectorAll(selector)];

  // Every query and mutation is confined to this page's root.
  $$('[data-explorer]').forEach(explorer => {
    const panels = [...explorer.querySelectorAll('[data-panel]')];
    const buttons = [...explorer.querySelectorAll('[data-choice]')];
    panels.forEach((panel,i) => { panel.hidden = i !== 0; });
    buttons.forEach(button => button.addEventListener('click', () => {
      buttons.forEach(item => item.setAttribute('aria-pressed', String(item === button)));
      panels.forEach(panel => { panel.hidden = panel.dataset.panel !== button.dataset.choice; });
      explorer.querySelector('[data-explorer-status]').textContent = `${button.textContent} selected. The explanation below has changed.`;
    }));
  });

  let selectedSemester = 'all';
  const normalize = value => value.normalize('NFD').replace(/[\u0300-\u036f]/g,'').toLowerCase();
  const filterReadings = () => {
    const search = normalize($('input[data-search]')?.value || '').trim();
    const theme = $('select[data-theme]')?.value || 'all';
    let count = 0;
    $$('[data-reading-row]').forEach(row => {
      const matches = normalize(row.dataset.search).includes(search) &&
        (selectedSemester === 'all' || row.dataset.semester === selectedSemester) &&
        (theme === 'all' || row.dataset.theme === theme);
      row.hidden = !matches;
      if (matches) count++;
    });
    const countLabel = $('[data-result-count]');
    if (countLabel) countLabel.textContent = `${count} ${count === 1 ? 'reading' : 'readings'} in view`;
    const empty = $('[data-empty]');
    if (empty) empty.hidden = count > 0;
  };
  $('input[data-search]')?.addEventListener('input', filterReadings);
  $('select[data-theme]')?.addEventListener('change', filterReadings);
  $$('[data-filter-semester]').forEach(button => button.addEventListener('click', () => {
    selectedSemester = button.dataset.filterSemester;
    $$('[data-filter-semester]').forEach(item => item.setAttribute('aria-pressed', String(item === button)));
    filterReadings();
  }));
  filterReadings();

  $$('[data-sort]').forEach(activity => {
    const selects = [...activity.querySelectorAll('select')];
    const status = activity.querySelector('[data-sort-status]');
    activity.querySelector('[data-check-sort]').addEventListener('click', () => {
      let correct = 0;
      selects.forEach(select => {
        const feedback = select.parentElement.querySelector('.sort-feedback');
        if (!select.value) {
          feedback.textContent = 'Choose a category, then check again.';
          select.removeAttribute('aria-invalid');
        } else {
          const matched = select.value === select.dataset.answer;
          correct += matched ? 1 : 0;
          feedback.textContent = `${matched ? 'Correct.' : 'Reconsider this choice.'} ${select.dataset.why}`;
          select.setAttribute('aria-invalid', String(!matched));
        }
      });
      status.textContent = `${correct} of ${selects.length} categories match the source. You can change your choices and check again.`;
    });
    activity.querySelector('[data-reset-sort]').addEventListener('click', () => {
      selects.forEach(select => {select.value='';select.removeAttribute('aria-invalid');});
      activity.querySelectorAll('.sort-feedback').forEach(item => {item.textContent='';});
      status.textContent = 'Categories reset.';
      selects[0].focus();
    });
  });

  const accessResult = $('[data-access-result]');
  if (accessResult) {
    const responses = {
      distance:'Distance: support a nearby fresh-food provider or help an existing shop add fresh options.',
      transport:'Transport: grocery trips or delivery can connect households with food.',
      cost:'Cost: a closer shop does not make food affordable by itself. The article calls for attention to poverty.',
      safety:'Safety: safer walking and transit routes can make existing stores more reachable.'
    };
    const updateAccess = () => {
      const rural = $('[data-setting]').value === 'rural';
      const intro = document.createElement('p');
      intro.textContent = rural ? 'Rural context: stores may be far apart and have too few customers to buy fresh stock cheaply.' : 'Urban context: even a short distance can be difficult without transport or a safe route.';
      const list = document.createElement('ul');
      const selected = $$('[data-barrier]:checked');
      selected.forEach(input => {const item=document.createElement('li');item.textContent=responses[input.value];list.append(item);});
      if (!selected.length) {const item=document.createElement('li');item.textContent='No barriers selected. This does not prove that food access is adequate; choose a barrier to examine a response.';list.append(item);}
      accessResult.replaceChildren(intro,list);
    };
    $('[data-setting]').addEventListener('change',updateAccess);
    $$('[data-barrier]').forEach(input=>input.addEventListener('change',updateAccess));
    updateAccess();
  }

  const surveyResponses = [
    'Born or raised · 26%: early experiences can continue to anchor belonging even after a person moves.',
    'Currently live · 22%: present routines and relationships can make a current residence feel like home.',
    'Lived longest · 18%: accumulated experiences can make time spent in a place meaningful.',
    'Extended family roots · 15%: family history can connect someone to a place beyond their present address.'
  ];
  $$('[data-survey]').forEach(button => button.addEventListener('click', () => {
    $$('[data-survey]').forEach(item=>item.setAttribute('aria-pressed',String(item===button)));
    $('[data-survey-result]').textContent = surveyResponses[Number(button.dataset.survey)];
  }));
})();
</script>
